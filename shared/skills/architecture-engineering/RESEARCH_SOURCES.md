**Content read (paraphrased)**

- Inspection types (ข้อ ๑๓): a major inspection every five years, and an annual inspection between major inspections following the inspector's annual plan.
- Minimum inspection scope (ข้อ ๑๗): structural stability (alterations and additions, changes in floor loads, change of use, change of construction or finishing materials, wear and deterioration, structural failure, foundation settlement); building systems (lifts, escalators, electrical, air-conditioning, water supply, wastewater drainage and treatment, stormwater drainage, waste management, ventilation, air and noise pollution control, fire protection systems including fire stairs and exits, exit signs, smoke control, emergency power, fire lift, alarm, extinguishing equipment, fire water supply and pumps, automatic suppression, lightning protection); evacuation performance of fire stairs, exit signs and alarm; building safety management plans.
- Criteria (ข้อ ๑๘): the inspector considers the building control law or other relevant law in force when the building was constructed, or safety standards of government institutions, the Council of Engineers, or the Architect Council.
- Report and follow-up (ข้อ ๑๙–๒๑): the inspector reports to the owner and recommends corrections for items that fail; for tall buildings, extra-large buildings and assembly buildings exempted from ministerial regulation ฉบับที่ ๓๓ (พ.ศ. ๒๕๓๕), fire-safety recommendations must be at least those in ฉบับที่ ๔๗ (พ.ศ. ๒๕๔๐) (these two regulations are referenced in the text only; their contents were not read). The owner submits the report to the local official each year, within 30 days before the anniversary of the existing certificate.
- Missing drawings (ข้อ ๒๐): if the building has no drawings, the owner must obtain or prepare inspection drawings covering the ข้อ ๑๗ items.
- Maintenance (ข้อ ๒๒): the owner arranges inspection and maintenance per the manufacturer or installer manuals, or the inspector's maintenance plan, and keeps records.
- Inspector independence (ข้อ ๑๕): an inspector may not inspect a building they or their spouse, employee or agent designed, supervised, built, or installed equipment in, or own or operate.
- Inspector registration: certificate valid two years; insurance of at least one million baht per claim and two million per year, covering at least three years (ข้อ ๔–๕).

**Content read (paraphrased; all as of 2552 and to be re-checked against current law)**

- "Local official" (เจ้าพนักงานท้องถิ่น) varies by area: Bangkok governor, municipal mayor, provincial administrative organisation head, Pattaya mayor, tambon administrative organisation head, or other local head designated by the Interior Minister (p. 4).
- "Building" covers houses, shops, factories, offices and other structures people can enter or use, plus listed structures such as bridges, jetties and walls adjoining public land (p. 4).
- "Alteration" (ดัดแปลง) means changing, adding, reducing or enlarging the shape, boundary, type, proportion, weight or area of a building's structure or parts. Example given: mounting a steel mast on a roof for mobile signals counts as an alteration because it adds weight to the structure (p. 6).
- Alterations that do not need a permit (p. 7) include replacing structure with materials of the same size, number and type, except reinforced concrete, prestressed concrete and structural steel; and changing non-structural parts without adding more than 10 percent weight to any part of the existing structure; and changing floor or roof area by up to 5 m² in total without changing the number of columns or beams. For a renovation, this means any change to reinforced concrete, prestressed or steel structure is not within the no-permit list on this page.
- Buildings with controlled use (p. 9): some types have no area threshold (warehouses, hotels, condominiums, hospitals); others apply from an area or power threshold (commercial from 80 m², factory from 5 horsepower of machinery, school from 80 m², assembly from 300 m², office from 300 m²).
- Permit application documents (pp. 10–11): application form; plans, site plan and specifications (5 sets); one set of calculations for public buildings, special buildings or buildings largely of permanent, fire-resistant materials; copies of the architect's and engineer's licences for professionally controlled buildings; land title documents; neighbour consent where building within 50 cm of the boundary or sharing a wall; owner's authority or company documents as applicable.
- Exemption from plans and calculations (p. 12): a residence of up to two storeys and up to 150 m², farm produce storage up to 100 m², livestock building up to 100 m², fences, gates and similar, and water towers up to 6 m high, when built within a tambon administrative organisation area that is not under a ban on certain building types; only a site plan and proof of land rights are then submitted.
- Notification route (p. 15): for activities allowed by notification, the designers must be senior-level (วุฒิ) engineers and architects. The page notes the risk that the local official can later order corrections, possibly including demolition of non-compliant parts.
- Inspection rules timeline (p. 20) and penalties (pp. 21, 29) summarise the S-001 regulation and the Act; use S-001 and the Act itself for requirements.
- Fire zone (p. 23): within an announced fire zone, construction, alteration, demolition or relocation is prohibited for 45 days from the fire, except temporary relief buildings and minimal repairs for temporary occupation.
